# Pulse by SBH

## The promise
A clear record of your routine.

Pulse helps people record their own substances, schedules, supply and observations. The brand should feel calm, precise and personal. Show the product doing useful work; keep health claims out of marketing.

## Name and signature
App Store name: **Pulse by SBH**. Subtitle: **Peptide logs & schedules**.
In-app wordmark: **pulse.** with a cyan dot. On first contact, onboarding, About and the website, use **by Science By Hugs**. Use Pulse in ordinary sentences. Pulse 2.0 is an engineering/version label.

## Mark
The rounded lowercase p and detached dot connect the home-screen icon to the wordmark. The open stem gives it a simple, readable silhouette. Use the supplied SVG geometry; allow clear space of at least one dot diameter. Do not add a heartbeat, syringe, medical cross, glow or enclosing border. Preview the mark at 32 and 48 pixels before using it in a new context.

- `assets/brand/pulse-icon.svg`: editable icon master, full square navy background.
- `assets/brand/pulse-icon.png`: 1024 × 1024 opaque app icon.
- `assets/brand/pulse-mark.svg`: transparent vector mark.

## Color and type
| Role | Color | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | #070F1E | Main canvas and icon |
| Elevated navy | #0A1727 | Sheets and navigation |
| Panel | #0E1B2C | Group related content |
| Cyan | #62CCFF | Primary actions, active states, signature dot |
| White | #F7FAFC | Main text |
| Muted | #9AABBA | Supporting text |
| Green | #63D6A3 | Completed states |
| Amber | #F4BE73 | Attention needed |

Use native system sans-serif typography. Wordmark is lowercase and bold; interface actions are sentence case. Titles 28, body 15, detail 12. Use weight 700 for emphasis; reserve heavier type for the wordmark. Keep decorative background shapes faint and behind content. Color must accompany a label or icon when conveying status.

## Product rules
Use the existing theme tokens. Minimum touch target is 44; page inset is 20. Give forms consistent sheet headers and reachable Close controls. One primary action per step. Show dates in a layout that survives long values and larger text. Keep error messages specific and actionable.

## Voice
Helpful, direct and restrained. Say “Log saved,” “Nothing scheduled today,” or “Couldn’t sync. Try again.” Use “7-day record” for descriptive tracking. Avoid judgments about adherence, optimal protocols, guaranteed privacy, medical outcomes or promises of safety. Describe exactly what a feature does.

## Launch presentation
Screenshot sequence: Today → Protocol → Timeline → Inventory → privacy controls. Use short captions: “See what’s due,” “Organize your routine,” “Keep a clear record,” “Track your supply,” “Choose what appears.” Capture actual screens from the release build with sample data; no fabricated features or personal health data. Landing-page headline: “A clear record of your routine.” Supporting line: “Track your schedules, logs and supply with Pulse by Science By Hugs.”

Before a public launch: validate the icon on iOS and Android, create release screenshots, align website/support/privacy naming, and complete a separate name/trademark clearance review. This guide and mark are original project artwork, not a trademark clearance finding.
