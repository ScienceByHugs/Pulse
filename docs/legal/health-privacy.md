# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Draft — not yet effective.

## Scope

This separate draft covers health-related data processed by Pulse by SBH. It supplements the Privacy Policy. Applicable state requirements and final wording must be reviewed for the confirmed operator and launch locations. Version: 2026-10-13; effective date pending publication.

## Categories and sources

Health data may include your entered substances/medications, quantities, routes, sites, schedules, completion/skips, inventory, sleep, energy, mood and notes, and associated dates, time zones and correction/schedule history. We receive this information from you and generate tracking records from your interactions. The current app does not obtain medical records from a clinician or import health-platform data.

## Purposes of collection

We process these records to provide the tracking, sync, reminders, history, inventory, check-in summaries and exports you request. We do not use these features to diagnose conditions, prescribe or choose treatments. We do not operate health-data advertising, sale or AI-training features in the reviewed app. We promote Pulse outside the app. The app has no advertising feature, and we do not sell personal information or disclose health records for advertising.

## Sharing and recipients

Supabase processes synced records for hosting and authentication. Authorized service personnel/providers may access limited information where needed to operate or secure the service or respond to support. You can choose to disclose information through calendar sync, detailed notifications or exports to recipients you select. Legal disclosures may occur where required. Confirm the final processor/recipient list and contracts before publishing.

## Consent and withdrawal

Where applicable law requires consent, collection or sharing beyond what is necessary to provide your requested service must wait for the appropriate consent. Acceptance of terms is not blanket consent to all health-data uses. Disable optional features to stop future optional processing; use deletion controls or the verified privacy contact to request removal or withdraw consent. Some core tracking functionality cannot operate without the records you ask it to process. This draft does not itself implement a consent mechanism.

## Access, deletion and appeals

Use Account & Privacy to export records or delete the account. For additional health-data access, deletion, withdrawal or appeal requests, contact legal@sciencebyhugs.com. Requests will be verified proportionately and processed within applicable legal deadlines. Deletion requests must be propagated to applicable processors/recipients where required; exported copies you independently share may be outside our control. Final request and appeal procedures must be established before publication.
