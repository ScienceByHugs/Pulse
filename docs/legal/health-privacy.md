# Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · Version 2026-10-20

## Scope

This Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy supplements the Privacy Policy for Pulse by SBH, operated by Anthony Charles Hugershoff under the Science By Hugs DBA/trade name. It explains the health-related information we collect, why we collect it, who receives it, and your controls and rights. It applies to the United States adult service, including beta testing. The Privacy Policy provides additional security, retention, provider and contact details.

## Categories and sources

Health data may include your entered substances/medications, quantities, routes, sites, schedules, completion/skips, inventory, sleep, energy, mood and notes, and associated dates, time zones and correction/schedule history. We receive this information from you and generate tracking records from your interactions. The current app does not obtain medical records from a clinician or import health-platform data.

## Purposes of collection

We collect and process the health-related records you choose to enter to provide the tracking, synchronization, reminders, history, inventory, check-in summaries and exports you request, and to maintain and secure those services. We do not use the records to diagnose, prescribe or choose treatment. We do not sell consumer health data, use it for advertising/marketing or AI-model training, disclose it to data brokers, or operate health-related advertising. Pulse has no in-app advertising. We do not infer a diagnosis or use health data to determine insurance, credit or employment eligibility. We do not use geofencing to identify or target visits to healthcare facilities.

## Sharing and recipients

Supabase and its infrastructure subprocessors process synced health records on our behalf for hosting, authentication, security, recovery and service operation, under instructions and protection obligations described in the Privacy Policy. Access by the owner or authorized support/service personnel is limited to what is needed for a requested service, support or security purpose. Cloudflare serves the public notices/admin portal; the static policy host does not receive your Pulse health records.

You may independently choose recipients through exports, calendar sync or detailed notifications. These choices can expose information to your calendar provider, operating-system notification display, recipients or people with access to your device/calendar. We do not send records to a clinician without your choice. Required legal disclosures and protected organizational transfers are described in the Privacy Policy. No independent health-data sale or advertising recipient is authorized. We require processors receiving health data to provide the same or equal applicable policy protections.

## Consent and withdrawal

Entering a record asks Pulse to process that record for the selected tracking feature. Core tracking cannot operate without the records you ask it to save. Optional check-ins, profile photos, reminders, calendars, biometric lock and exports are choices; declining an optional feature does not prevent core tracking. System permissions are requested where needed.

We will obtain separate, specific consent before collecting or sharing consumer health data beyond what is necessary for your requested service when applicable law requires it. Consent to collection does not by itself authorize unrelated sharing or sale, and a policy notice or terms acceptance is not a substitute for required consent. No sale of health data is offered or authorized by these policies.

You can withdraw an optional permission/choice in Pulse or device settings, stop new entries, delete the account, or contact legal@sciencebyhugs.com to withdraw consent or request removal. Withdrawal stops the relevant future processing; it does not reverse earlier lawful processing or independently shared copies. We explain any core feature that cannot operate following a requested restriction.

## Access, deletion and appeals

Use You → Account & Privacy for export/deletion, or contact legal@sciencebyhugs.com for access, correction, confirmation of processing, information about health-data recipients, withdrawal or appeal. Email support@sciencebyhugs.com to request deletion without the app. We proportionately verify ownership and any representative authority, acknowledge requests as described in the Privacy Policy, and respond within applicable legal deadlines without discrimination.

We communicate applicable deletion/withdrawal requests to our processors and other recipients where legally required, including appropriate recovery-copy handling. If we deny a request or retain a limited legal/security record, we explain the reason and available appeal process unless legally prohibited. Appeal to legal@sciencebyhugs.com with “Privacy appeal.” You can also contact the relevant state attorney general or privacy authority. The Privacy Policy describes retention criteria and independent export/calendar copies.
