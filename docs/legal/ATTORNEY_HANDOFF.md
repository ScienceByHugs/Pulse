## Actual product behavior

Pulse records user-entered substances, amounts, administration methods, routines, inventory and activity; it offers user-configured reminders, calendars, optional check-ins, history and exports. It does not prescribe or recommend a dose. Previously removed calculators stay excluded. Eligibility is self-confirmation, not verified identity/location. PDFs and optional substance photos are on-device; profile photos and account records use Supabase. An account deletion flow exists; newly changed local cleanup needs native QA.

The approved 30-day unconfirmed-account cleanup is prepared as an owner-operated tool. It is not deployed or scheduled and must not be described as running. The privacy-request runbook is prepared; verification/deadline/retention rules need adoption and review.
