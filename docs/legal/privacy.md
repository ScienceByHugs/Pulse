## Information you provide

We collect your account email and authentication information. Optional profile information includes your name, contact phone number and selected profile photo. You may enter protocol and substance names, categories, amounts, units, routes, schedules, dates, injection-site records, completion/skip history, inventory quantities, container dates and lot numbers. Optional daily check-ins include sleep, energy, mood and notes. These records may reveal sensitive health information.

## Feedback and app administration

Optional in-app feedback includes the message and category you submit, app version, build number, platform and submission/status timestamps. Your tracking records, photos, check-ins and diagnostic logs are not attached. Your verified account email is shown with a ticket only if you choose to allow follow-up. Avoid including health records, passwords or other sensitive details in feedback. Feedback is linked to your account for My feedback, export and account deletion. Interrupted submissions retain their original message and request identifier in private device storage until confirmed or your account is deleted. The private admin portal shows aggregate account/signup counts and volunteered feedback; its endpoints do not expose individual profiles or health tracking records. It uses a separately approved admin account with authenticator verification. Minimal administrator status-change audit records contain ticket identifiers and statuses, not feedback messages or health records.

## Information generated by the service

We create account and record identifiers, timestamps, local dates, time-zone information, correction history, schedule versions and sync/cancellation records to operate the service. Our hosting and authentication providers may process connection information such as IP addresses, request metadata and security logs. A time zone is not GPS location. The reviewed app does not request precise location or integrate HealthKit or Health Connect.

## How we use information

We use information to authenticate accounts, save and synchronize your records, display your history, manage inventory, provide user-configured reminders and calendar entries, generate requested exports, support you, and secure and maintain the service. Check-in summaries describe your entries; they do not establish medical causation. Optional profile photos and check-ins are not required for basic tracking.

## Promotional email plans

We send necessary account, verification, password-reset, security and support messages. Promotional email is not an active in-app feature. If we offer it later, enrollment will be separate and optional, with an unsubscribe method. Declining promotions will not prevent use of core tracking features. We do not use health records to target marketing.

## Providers and disclosures

Supabase processes account authentication, synced database records and private profile-photo storage, including email-delivery subprocessors used for account messages. Cloudflare serves our public policy pages and separate admin portal and processes connection information needed for delivery and security. Expo supplies app development libraries/tooling; Pulse does not send tracking records to Expo for advertising or AI training. Apple and Google distribute the mobile apps and provide operating-system services. Their own platform/account processing is covered by their policies.

We limit service-provider access to the data needed for our instructions, support, security and legal obligations. We require providers that receive personal data on our behalf to maintain confidentiality, purpose limits, security and retention/deletion safeguards providing the same or equal protection required by this policy and applicable Apple/Google requirements. Providers may use subprocessors under equivalent protection obligations. We do not authorize independent advertising or health-data monetization by our processors.

The owner and authorized service personnel may access the minimum needed to operate the service, resolve a request or protect it. The admin portal displays aggregate account counts and volunteered feedback, not a directory of health records. We may disclose data when legally required, to protect rights or safety, or in an organizational transfer subject to continued protection and required notice/consent. We do not sell personal information or consumer health data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, use health data for advertising or AI-model training, or run in-app advertising, data-broker or advertising-tracking features. We promote the app separately without using your health records.

## Your sharing choices

Calendar sync is optional. Calendar entries can be accessible to your calendar provider and anyone you allow to access that calendar. Private notification titles are enabled by default; names and quantities can appear if you enable detailed text. Widgets display due counts. PDF reports are generated on your device and are not uploaded to Pulse storage; temporary native report files are removed after sharing finishes. Saved/shared exports and calendar copies are outside Pulse and may be retained by their recipients. Review sharing destinations before sending sensitive records.

## Permissions and device storage

Photo access selects an optional avatar; the app uploads a resized JPEG rather than your entire photo library. Notification, calendar and biometric-lock features use optional platform permissions. Pulse receives a biometric authentication result, not your face or fingerprint template. Native sessions, cached routine records and pending logs use platform secure storage. The web app uses browser storage for sessions and local state. See our Device Storage Notice for more detail. Optional substance photos are resized and stored on this device only, separately from the backend profile photo. On native devices they use private secure storage; on web they use browser storage. They can be replaced or removed and included in an account export from this device. Unconfirmed creation requests retain their identifiers and original input in private device storage so interrupted saves can be retried without duplicating records. Successful requests are cleared; remaining requests can be included in an account export from that device.

## Retention and deletion

We retain account/profile information and synced protocols, logs, inventory, check-ins and feedback while your account exists, unless you remove a supported record or request deletion. Correction and schedule history remain with the account to preserve your tracking history. An unconfirmed signup may remain until it is removed through account-support operations; no automatic 30-day cleanup is currently promised.

Confirmed in-app account deletion removes the authentication account, associated active application records, submitted feedback and stored profile photo. Account-scoped device queues, cached records and local photos are also cleared by the app; if device cleanup reports an error, follow the displayed retry/support instructions. Uninstalling or signing out alone does not delete server records. You may also request account and associated-data deletion without installing or using Pulse by emailing support@sciencebyhugs.com; see Account & Data Deletion.

Provider recovery backups, object-recovery copies and security/operational logs can remain after active records are deleted until their configured rotation or deletion process completes. Their periods depend on the provider, plan, data category and security/legal purpose; Pulse has not verified a single fixed period for every such copy and does not promise immediate or seven-day erasure from all backups. Restricted recovery data is not used for new tracking or marketing; applicable deletion requests are passed to processors, and previously completed deletions must be reapplied if recovery restores affected records.

We retain only the minimum request/audit information needed to verify and document a request, resolve a dispute, prevent abuse or satisfy a specific legal obligation. Minimal feedback-status audit identifiers/statuses can remain without the deleted account actor, message or email. Retention is limited to the period needed for the stated purpose or required by law; we review and remove information when that purpose ends. If a retention exception prevents full deletion, we explain the category, purpose and applicable period or determining criteria unless prohibited by law. Exports, screenshots and calendar copies you or recipients independently retain are outside Pulse; you control those copies.

## Your controls and rights

You can review and update supported profile/record fields, export your account data and delete your account in You → Account & Privacy. Remove an optional profile photo, turn off reminders/calendar sync or biometric lock, and revoke photo, notification, calendar or biometric permissions in device settings. Declining optional permissions does not prevent core tracking; only the dependent optional feature is unavailable. Stop entering health information or request deletion if you no longer want Pulse to process it for tracking.

Depending on applicable law, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, portability, deletion, withdrawal of consent, restriction, information about recipients, or appeal a denied privacy request. Contact legal@sciencebyhugs.com, or support@sciencebyhugs.com for deletion, using your account email and request type. We verify ownership proportionately without requesting a password or health records by email, and can explain a secure recovery/verification route if you cannot sign in. An authorized representative may contact us with appropriate authority.

We aim to acknowledge requests within three business days and respond within applicable legal deadlines, giving any permitted extension and reasons. You may appeal by emailing legal@sciencebyhugs.com with “Privacy appeal”; we review and explain the result and any applicable regulator complaint route. You may contact your state attorney general or other competent privacy authority. We do not discriminate for exercising privacy rights. Withdrawal does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Accepting terms or reading a policy is not blanket consent to new or optional health-data uses; any legally required separate consent must be obtained before that use.

## Security and international processing

Pulse uses HTTPS for service requests, account ownership restrictions, private profile-photo access and platform secure storage for native sessions and account-scoped local records. Web browser storage has different protections and depends on your browser/device. Server/service personnel with authorized access can process synced information; Pulse is not an end-to-end encrypted service. Protect your device, account and exported copies. No system is perfectly secure.

The primary backend region was verified as us-east-2 (United States) on October 12, 2026. This is not a promise that every service activity remains there. Supabase may process support, account metadata, operational logs/telemetry and content delivery in other countries, using its contractual transfer and security safeguards. Cloudflare distributes public pages through a global network and processes connection information for delivery/security. Platform, email and support providers may also process information internationally. We do not claim HIPAA coverage, a business-associate relationship or regulatory certification for Pulse merely because a provider offers relevant services.

## Children and changes

The effective date and policy version are displayed with this policy. A policy update does not retroactively authorize a new purpose, broader health-data disclosure or reduced protection. These notices describe current practices; Apple App Privacy and Google Data Safety disclosures must also accurately reflect them.
