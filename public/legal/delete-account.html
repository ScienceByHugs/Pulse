Account & Data Deletion

Draft for operator and legal review. Not yet published or effective.

Delete inside Pulse

Open Pulse, sign in and go to You → Account & Privacy. Choose Delete account & data and confirm. The server removes your profile photo and authentication account; associated synced protocol, item, inventory, tracking, correction, schedule-history and check-in records are removed through account deletion. The deletion must be confirmed by the server; an error is not a completed deletion.

What can remain

Copies you exported, screenshots, recipient copies and third-party calendar entries remain outside Pulse. Remove Pulse calendar events before deletion if desired. Provider backups/security logs may persist for their configured retention periods, and narrowly required legal records may be retained. Confirm and publish actual durations/categories before launch; do not promise immediate erasure from all backups.

Export before deleting

You can choose Export data in Account & Privacy before deletion. Store the export securely. Deletion is permanent for active account records and cannot be undone through the app.
