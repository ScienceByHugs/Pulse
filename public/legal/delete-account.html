Account & Data Deletion

Effective October 20, 2026 · Version 2026-10-20

Delete inside Pulse

Open Pulse, sign in and go to You → Account & Privacy. Choose Delete account & data and confirm the destructive action. Deletion removes the account, not merely disables it. The server removes the stored profile photo and authentication account; associated active profile, protocol, item, inventory, tracking, correction, schedule-history, check-in and submitted-feedback records are removed through account deletion. No purchase, subscription cancellation, phone call or support approval is required for this in-app request. The app must receive server confirmation; an error is not a completed deletion. The app then clears account-scoped local queues, caches, retry records and photos. If local cleanup reports a problem, follow the displayed retry/support instructions.

Request deletion without the app

You can initiate deletion of your Pulse account and associated data without installing, reinstalling or opening Pulse. Email support@sciencebyhugs.com with the subject “Pulse account deletion” and your account email. You do not need to send a password, health records or identity document in the initial email. We will explain a proportionate secure verification process before deleting data, especially if you cannot sign in. We do not reject a request solely because you no longer have the app. Privacy/legal questions: legal@sciencebyhugs.com.

What can remain

Account deletion removes associated active Pulse records and stored profile photos after confirmation. Copies you export or share, screenshots, and third-party calendar/recipient copies are outside Pulse; remove them from their destinations if desired. Deleting local app storage or uninstalling alone does not remove server records.

Provider recovery backups, object-recovery copies and operational/security logs may persist until the provider’s configured rotation/deletion cycle completes; the period depends on provider, plan, data type and purpose. Pulse does not promise immediate or a fixed seven-day erasure of all copies. Applicable deletion requests are communicated to processors and completed deletions must be reapplied if recovery restores affected records.

Only the minimum privacy-request/audit, security, dispute or legally required information is retained for the purpose and period necessary or required by law. Minimal feedback-status audit identifiers/statuses may remain without the deleted account actor, message or email. We review retention and explain any exception, category and period or determining criteria unless prohibited by law. Retained recovery/legal data is not used for new tracking or advertising. The Privacy Policy gives the full retention criteria.

Export before deleting

You can choose Export data in Account & Privacy before deletion. Store the export securely. Deletion is permanent for active account records and cannot be undone through the app.
