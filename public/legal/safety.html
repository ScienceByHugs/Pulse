Health & Safety Notice

Effective October 20, 2026 · Version 2026-10-20

Tracking boundaries

Pulse by SBH is a personal record of the routine you enter. Pulse is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Do not use it as clinical decision support or to decide whether a substance or routine is appropriate for you.

Clinical decisions

Pulse does not prescribe, recommend doses, choose an injection site or establish whether a peptide, medication or supplement is safe or effective. Consult a qualified clinician or pharmacist before starting, stopping or changing a substance or routine. Follow their instructions and the product’s lawful labeling. A substance name in your records does not imply approval or endorsement.

Reminders and inventory

Do not rely on Pulse as your only reminder or clinical record. Reminders and sync can be interrupted. Supply estimates and user-entered expiry/use-by dates do not assess product potency, sterility, storage suitability or stability. Logging or skipping an entry does not determine what you should do next.

Emergency use

Pulse is not monitored for emergencies. If you suspect an emergency, contact your local emergency service or seek immediate professional care. Check-in notes are not a clinician messaging service.

Reports

Reports reproduce selected user-entered information. They may omit unavailable historical details and are not verified clinical assessments. Review them before sharing.
